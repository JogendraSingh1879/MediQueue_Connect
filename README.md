MediQueue Connect is a full-stack healthcare consultation and digital clinic management platform built for India’s next generation of doctors, clinics, and patients. The app enables verified doctors to manage online and in-clinic consultations, patient records, queue flow, payments, video consultations, prescriptions, staff attendance, clinic inventory, medicine stock, and medical knowledge references from one unified dashboard.

For patients, MediQueue Connect simplifies the entire journey: discover verified specialists, book appointments, pay securely, track live queue status, join video consultations, upload reports, and access digital prescriptions anytime.

What makes MediQueue Connect different is its clinic-first approach. Most healthcare apps focus mainly on doctor discovery, teleconsultation, medicine delivery, or lab bookings. MediQueue Connect goes deeper by helping doctors run their actual clinic operations, including staff, attendance, stock, medicine expiry alerts, repeat patient history, and consultation workflow.

It is not just a booking platform; it is a digital operating system for modern medical practice.

Designed with secure records, role-based dashboards, audit logs, and scalable architecture, MediQueue Connect bridges the gap between patient convenience and doctor productivity.
